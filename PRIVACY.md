# GhostIDE Privacy Policy

Ghost Developer Studio · Oakdale, Tennessee
Contact: (423) 408-2150

GhostIDE is an offline-first code editor with an AI
assistant that runs on your device. This policy describes
exactly what the app does with information, in plain
language.

## What GhostIDE collects

Nothing. GhostIDE has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, and no tracking. We do not receive your
code, your files, your chats, or any usage data, because
the app never sends them to us. There is no server of
ours for them to go to.

## What stays on your device

- Your projects and files, in the workspace you choose.
- The AI model files you download, stored in the app's
  private storage.
- Your settings, chat history, snippets, and local file
  history, all stored locally by the app.

Deleting the app deletes this data with it.

## Network use, feature by feature

The app works offline. These optional features use the
network only when you use them:

- Model download: the app downloads AI model files from
  Hugging Face. Hugging Face's servers see the download
  request (like any file download). Their privacy policy
  covers their side of that connection.
- Update check (desktop): the app may fetch a small
  version file from GitHub to tell you an update exists.
  It never downloads or installs an update by itself.
- Pair mode: connects only to another device running
  GhostIDE on your own local network, at your direction.
- Encrypted backup: produces an encrypted file on your
  device. Where you store it (for example your own cloud
  drive) is your choice; we never receive it.
- Voice dictation: uses your device's built-in speech
  recognition. On many Android phones that service is
  provided by Google and may process audio according to
  Google's policies. GhostIDE itself records nothing;
  the microphone permission is requested only when you
  tap the microphone button.

## Permissions

- Internet: for the optional features above.
- Microphone: only for voice dictation, only while you
  use it.
- Notifications: only to show model download progress.

## Children

GhostIDE is a developer tool, not directed at children
under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this policy
will be updated and the version it applies to will be
noted here before that version ships.
